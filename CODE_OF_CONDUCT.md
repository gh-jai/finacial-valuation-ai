# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, precise, patient, and open to correction. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Respect confidentiality and attribution boundaries. Disclose material conflicts of interest when evaluating financial claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate plagiarism, copyright violations, and knowingly misleading financial representations are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security contact channel. Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants in proportion to the behavior. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
