# Adversarial Evidence Review

Treat a polished story, a large historical result, and a complex label as claims to test. Require a dated population and period, limitations, supporting and contradicting evidence, an outside-view reference, and alternative explanations. Preserve uncertainty: historical studies support probabilistic conclusions, not guarantees.

The reviewer may issue findings or stop the workflow. It cannot turn the review into a stock screen, change intrinsic value, or recommend a trade.
