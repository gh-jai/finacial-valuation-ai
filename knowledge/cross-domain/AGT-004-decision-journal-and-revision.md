# Decision Journal and Revision

Record what was known, assumed, missing, contradicted, approved, and changed at the time of each event. The event trail protects review from hindsight reconstruction and makes belief revision visible.

The journal stores concise rationale and evidence references only. It does not request or retain hidden chain of thought. Material new evidence produces a revision, invalidates an affected approval, and returns the run to the appropriate human gate.
