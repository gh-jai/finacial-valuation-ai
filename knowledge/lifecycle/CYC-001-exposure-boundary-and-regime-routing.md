# Cycle Exposure Boundary and Regime Routing

Use company evidence to connect an external economic or commodity driver to revenue, costs, margins, reinvestment, capital, returns, leverage, and financing. An industry label, one weak period, or a large price move is insufficient. Clear M3, M4, and M5 boundaries before selecting M6.

Classify the regime as established recurring, unstable, structural break, or insufficiently evidenced. A structural break requires a dated mechanism, affected inputs, and counterevidence. Stop when the exposure or life-cycle route is unsupported.
