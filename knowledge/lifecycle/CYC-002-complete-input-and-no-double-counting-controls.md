# Complete Input and No-double-counting Controls

A governed treatment updates the full linked vector, not earnings alone. Peak or trough reinvestment, capital, leverage, return, or financing assumptions cannot survive unchanged beside normalized operating income. A normalized trough base cannot also receive a separate recovery-growth effect.

M6 exposes rather than hides cycle views. It does not predict the dates and magnitudes of future cycles or mix a non-consensus macro view into company value without traceability.
