# Decline Boundary Routing and Normalized Base

M5 requires persistent company and sector evidence rather than one weak period. Reversibility and distress are separate judgments: the first concerns the operating decline, while the second concerns fixed obligations, liquidity, and financing access.

The base period removes discontinued operations and completed asset sales, then updates cash, face and market debt, invested capital, and fixed obligations to one valuation date. Stop when a young, growth, ordinary mature, cyclical, or commodity route has not been cleared.
