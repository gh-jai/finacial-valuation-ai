# Reversibility and Turnaround Alternatives

Recovery history, healthy peers, management-specific underperformance, sector structure, and macro cyclicality inform reversibility. A reversible route uses two complete operating valuations. Their revenues, margins, reinvestment, and rates are never averaged into a hybrid forecast.

The approved probability of management or policy change weights the two final values on the same basis and date.
