# Growth-company Boundary and Normalized Base

A growth company has cleared the young-company boundary through demonstrated commercial activity and meaningful operating evidence, yet a material share of its value still depends on growth assets. Classification uses several reviewed facts rather than one threshold.

Fast-changing revenue, operating income, capital, cash, debt, and NOL balances must be normalized to the valuation date. Stale statements or growth-creating expenses require explicit adjustments and evidence.

## Controls

Stop if the M3 boundary is uncleared, the current base is stale without reconciliation, or M3 and M4 forecasts are applied to the same narrative.
