# Revenue Scale and Growth Fade

Percentage growth must be translated into absolute revenue and, when market data is available, implied market share. The path should reflect market growth, competitive entry, the company's own scaling history, and mature participants.

Growth need not decline mechanically every year, but a plateau or increase requires a separate reviewed narrative assertion. Small, volatile, stale, or accounting-distorted base values cannot justify blind historical extrapolation.

## Controls

Reject unbounded scale, revenue above the addressable market, unsupported plateaus, or growth rates selected to reach a desired value.
