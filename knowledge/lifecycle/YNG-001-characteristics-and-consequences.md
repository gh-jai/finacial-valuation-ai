# Young-company Characteristics and Valuation Consequences

Young companies combine limited history, small or absent revenue, operating losses, private-capital dependence, material failure risk, illiquidity, and potentially unequal equity claims. Classification must be explicit because historical extrapolation is weak and most value lies in future operations.

## Decision rules

Use at least two observable young-company indicators; distinguish early businesses from established high-growth companies; keep employee options and claim rights visible.

## Failure modes

Mechanical historical forecasting, ignoring survival risk, or treating all equity claims as economically identical.
