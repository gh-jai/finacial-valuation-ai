# Narrative-driven Revenue Forecasting

Top-down forecasts derive revenue from a defined total-market path and company share. Bottom-up forecasts derive units, revenue, operating income, and capacity investment from capacity, utilization, price, variable cost, and fixed cost. Both retain M2 assertion and evidence traces.

## Controls

Choose the method before viewing value; reconcile capacity with top-down demand when both are available; keep alternatives separate.
