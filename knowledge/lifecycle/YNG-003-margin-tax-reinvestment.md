# Target Margins, Taxes, Reinvestment, and Consistency

Forecasts specify a defensible mature margin and an explicit path from losses. NOL balances shelter positive operating income before marginal taxes apply. Revenue growth requires reinvestment through a sales-to-capital ratio, optionally shifted earlier by a documented lag.

## Controls

Check implied return on capital against the narrative, industry economics, and cost of capital; reject unsupported growth or an implicit margin jump.

## Evidence

Implements `CLM-YNG-016` through `CLM-YNG-019`, printed pages 283–298.
