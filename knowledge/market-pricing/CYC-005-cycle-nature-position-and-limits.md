# Cycle Nature Position and Limits

Record possible outcomes, tendencies, causal feedback, and present position. Recurrence can support comparisons around a midpoint, but timing, speed, duration, amplitude, and ordering vary. No market band predicts a turning date or guarantees an outcome.

Extreme conditions can increase the consequences of decisions built for an unsustainable environment. This observation remains evidence for review posture, not a transaction instruction.
