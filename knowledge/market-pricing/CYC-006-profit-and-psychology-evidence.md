# Profit and Psychology Evidence

Economic conditions affect companies unevenly through operating leverage, financial leverage, management, industry structure, and technology. Profit evidence therefore remains company-specific.

Psychology can amplify fundamentals into optimism or pessimism. Record its direction and degree with dated evidence and limitations; it cannot alter FCFF or WACC without a separate financial bridge.
