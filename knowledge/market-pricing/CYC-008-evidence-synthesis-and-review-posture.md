# Evidence Synthesis and Review Posture

Use economic, company-profit, psychology and valuation, risk-attitude, and credit evidence. Missing data is explicit; stale evidence does not support extremes or high confidence. Price is observed only after intrinsic value.

The output is defensive, balanced, opportunity, or insufficient-evidence review. It never changes intrinsic value, predicts timing, chooses portfolio weights, or issues a buy or sell instruction.
