# Narrative Pre-work and Business Definition

## Principle

A valuation narrative starts with a defensible definition of what the company does, whom it serves, where it competes, and how management and history shaped its current position. The definition controls the relevant market, competitors, and growth ceiling.

## Decision rules

1. State products, customers, geography, life-cycle stage, and valuation date.
2. Describe market size, growth, structure, current and potential competitors, entry barriers, and advantages.
3. Use history as evidence, not as a mechanical forecast.
4. Record supporting and contradicting evidence with stable identifiers.

## Failure modes

Undefined market boundaries, omitted potential competitors, unsupported management claims, hindsight narratives, or private evidence copied into a public artifact.
