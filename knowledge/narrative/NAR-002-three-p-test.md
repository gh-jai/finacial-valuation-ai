# Possible, Plausible, Probable Review

## Principle

The 3P test separates a coherent path from demonstrated small-scale viability and from evidence that the story can scale while defenses endure. Each assessment records a result, supporting and contradicting evidence, and reviewer reasoning.

## Decision rules

- A failed possibility test stops valuation of that narrative.
- Plausibility needs evidence beyond internal aspiration.
- Probability needs scale and competitive-defense evidence.
- Uncertainty remains explicit; it is not silently upgraded to a pass.

## Failure modes

Unsupported labels, circular evidence, no counterevidence, or treating possible as probable.
