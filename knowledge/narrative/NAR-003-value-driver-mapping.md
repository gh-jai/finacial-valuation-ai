# Narrative to Value-driver Mapping

## Mapping contract

Revenue growth reflects market size, growth, share, price, and volume. Margins reflect unit economics, scale, and competition. Reinvestment reflects capital needed for growth. Cost of capital and failure risk reflect operating, financing, and country exposure. Terminal growth reflects a reviewed stable state.

Every mapped input retains its assertion ID, rationale, and evidence references. Every material assertion is mapped, observable-only, or explicitly classified as an unquantified limitation.

## Failure modes

Orphan inputs, unmapped material assertions, duplicate input names, unsupported terminal assumptions, and dropping failure risk because the M1 engine does not directly probability-adjust value.
