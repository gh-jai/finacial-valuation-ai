# Parsimony and Two-way Consistency

## Principle

A useful model contains enough structure to express the material story without inputs that cannot be explained. Audit both directions: story to number and number back to story.

## Controls

- Trace every material input to an assertion and evidence.
- Classify every material assertion as mapped, observable-only, or an unquantified limitation.
- Reject complexity that adds no decision-relevant distinction.
- Surface inconsistencies for human resolution rather than averaging them away.
