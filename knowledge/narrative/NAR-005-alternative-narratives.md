# Alternative Narratives and Conditional Value

## Principle

Value is conditional on a narrative and its inputs. Credible alternatives remain separate versioned objects, receive independent 3P reviews, produce independent FCFF contracts, and are valued separately.

## Controls

Never average, merge, or overwrite alternatives before valuation. Compare their credibility, assumptions, values, and evidence after separate calculations.

## Evidence

Implements `CLM-NAR-019` through `CLM-NAR-021`, Chapter 9, printed pages 128–150.
