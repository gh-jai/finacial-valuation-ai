# Feedback Loops and Narrative Revision

## Principle

Informed disagreement and new evidence can improve a narrative. A revision preserves the prior version, triggering evidence, classification, changed assertions and inputs, and value delta.

## Classification

Use `tweak`, `shift`, `change`, or `break` only with reviewer reasoning. The terminology continues beyond the core Chapters 6–10 scope into printed pages 167–183; this boundary must remain visible.

## Controls

Never rewrite history in place. A break invalidates the old business model; a change alters the core story; a shift changes contours while the core remains; a tweak is limited.
