# Risk Attitude Credit and Distress Separation

Assess risk compensation, issuance behavior, lender eagerness, underwriting, terms, and refinancing access as separate dated dimensions. Broad credit can be abundant while one issuer remains financially weak.

Qualitative credit or risk labels have no direct numeric valuation effect. A documented financial relationship may feed a financing path; discrete distress, probability, recovery, and forced-sale effects go once to `WFL-DST-001`.
