# Divestiture Integrity and Orderly Liquidation

Each sale records the asset, year, capital removed, operating contribution removed, gross proceeds, direct costs, taxes, net proceeds, and sale condition. Forecast earnings cannot retain a disposed asset, and proceeds cannot be added twice.

For irreversible low distress, a separately discounted, non-urgent liquidation program is compared with the independently completed status-quo going concern. Full liquidation has no terminal value; partial liquidation remains a governed divestiture inside retained operations.
