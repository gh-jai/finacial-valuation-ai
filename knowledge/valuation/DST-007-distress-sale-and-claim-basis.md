# Distress-sale Recovery and Claim Basis

Recovery uses exactly one primary method: a disclosed going-concern haircut, existing-asset earnings power without growth, or adjusted book assets with economic impairment and forced-sale discounts. Growth assets, costs, perimeter, and beneficiary remain explicit.

Going-concern and sale values share one operating-asset, firm, equity, or named-claim basis. Current cash, market debt, senior claims, hybrids, and option claims are dated and bridged once after aggregation.
