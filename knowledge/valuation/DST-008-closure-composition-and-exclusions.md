# Closure Composition and Method Exclusions

The status-quo case selects finite life, a stabilized smaller company, or a reviewed negative perpetuity. A terminal FCFF is rebuilt from terminal revenue, margin, tax, return, reinvestment, and cost of capital; full orderly liquidation remains a separate alternative.

M5 reuses M1 cumulative discounting, M4 operating consistency, and M3 separate survival arithmetic. Relative valuation, simulation, modified expected cash flow, APV, and equity-as-option results are excluded and never stacked onto M5 intrinsic value.
