# Operating-margin Convergence

The current margin, target margin, convergence start, convergence end, and path are explicit. The target is supported by unit economics, operating leverage, mature peers, and the competitive narrative.

The path may rise or fall, but it cannot silently jump to the target. Cash taxes and NOL use are recomputed from the resulting operating-income series.
