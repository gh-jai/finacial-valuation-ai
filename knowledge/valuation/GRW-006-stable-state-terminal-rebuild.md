# Stable-state Terminal Rebuild

The mature year is explicit and normally no later than year ten. Terminal revenue, after-tax operating income, reinvestment, and FCFF are rebuilt from stable drivers. The last high-growth FCFF is not merely grown one more period.

Stable growth stays below the terminal cost of capital and the long-run ceiling. Return on capital, excess return, reinvestment, margin, tax, leverage, and risk describe the same competitive state. Terminal-value share is disclosed because it is often material.
