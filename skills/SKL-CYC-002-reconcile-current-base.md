# Reconcile the Current Operating and Financing Base

Align revenue, margin, income, reinvestment, capital, returns, cash, debt, obligations, leverage, funding cost, currency, and period to the valuation date. Reconcile completed disposals, acquisitions, restructurings, and accounting discontinuities.
